The COP Angel advantage

One intelligent system
Four meaningful benefits

Conventional heating controls aren't always equipped to optimise an entire low-temperature system. COP Angel brings the heating circuit, individual rooms and heat pump together.

Automatic commissioning

Designed to simplify balancing and system setup, reducing manual tuning during installation.

Room-by-room comfort

Independent room targets with coordinated control across radiators and underfloor heating.

Optimised efficiency

Continuously seek the lowest practical flow temperature while meeting the home's heating needs.

Straightforward installation

Smart valves, actuators and a wireless control box, designed to work together as one system.

A practical approach

Smarter control
Familiar heating systems

COP Angel’s approach replaces standard radiator valves and underfloor heating actuators with smart motorised components, wirelessly linked to a control box connected to the heat pump. The system is designed to balance heat delivery automatically and coordinate room comfort with lower operating temperatures.

Intelligence behind it

Every home is different. So are the people who live in it. COP Angel is being developed around a bespoke, AI-led approach that brings together the needs of your heating system, your rooms and the way you use them.

Our AI-enabled platform is designed to adapt heating to your home and your daily routines, continually seeking better efficiency while protecting the comfort that matters to you.

A planned subscription model will support ongoing optimisation, with the aim of delivering energy savings that outweigh the subscription cost and create lasting value for your household.
