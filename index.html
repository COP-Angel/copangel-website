The COP Angel advantage

One intelligent system
Four meaningful benefits

Conventional heating controls aren't equipped to optimise an entire low-temperature system. COP Angel brings the heating circuit, individual rooms and heat pump together.

Automatic commissioning

The commissioning workflow brings system setup, emitter balancing and saved reference temperatures together in one engineer-facing view.

Room-by-room comfort

See individual room temperatures and targets, then explore the Angel Room view showing which room is guiding the home's heating demand.

Optimised efficiency

Explore energy use, heat delivered and the operating conditions behind the numbers, with a clearer view of how the system is performing.

Straightforward installation

Smart valves, actuators and a wireless control box work together as one system, supported by an engineering cockpit for setup and oversight.

A practical approach

Smarter control
Familiar heating systems

COP Angel’s approach replaces standard radiator valves and underfloor heating actuators with smart motorised components, wirelessly linked to a control box connected to the heat pump. The system is designed to balance heat delivery automatically during commissioning and coordinate room comfort with lower operating temperatures. This delivers significantly lower run costs and carbon savings for the client.

Our founding team

Experience behind the innovation

Our founding team brings together AI early adoption, deep heat pump technical expertise, manufacturing know-how, and the drive to bring this innovation to life.

COP Angel founder, Director and inventor Mike Kellett brings over 15 years in air source heat pump system design and installation, alongside an entrepreneurial background spanning 30 years. Co-founder and Director Chris Davidson brings more than 25 years in ground source heat pump system design. Commercial Director Richard Naylor adds senior leadership experience gained at Procter & Gamble and extensive knowledge of working with manufacturers.

Rich Davis, CEO and co-founder, combines a background in IT and telecoms with experience in renewable energy since 2010, alongside the practical problem-solving and teamwork demanded by his earlier career as a technical rescue firefighter and member of the UK International Search and Rescue Team. Engineer Eric Johnson brings inventive thinking and hands-on expertise to our early prototypes, combining hardware integration, chipsets and software programming to turn ambitious ideas into working systems.

Together, this leadership team combines heating engineering, digital technology, manufacturing, software development and commercial delivery, with AI central to its approach - and a shared passion to bring COP Angel from working prototypes to market.
