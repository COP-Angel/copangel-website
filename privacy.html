Enquiries

When you submit a form, the information you provide is processed by FormSubmit and forwarded to our inbox so we can respond. This can include your name, email address, selected interests, optional company or organisation and telephone number, and message. FormSubmit states that it retains submissions for 30 days. Information delivered to our inbox is handled separately by COP Angel.

Product and launch updates are optional. We include your choice in the enquiry email. You can ask us to stop sending updates by contacting info@copangel.com.

You can read the FormSubmit privacy notice. Its submission page may use Google's spam protection.

COP Angel AI assistant

The optional chat assistant answers questions using COP Angel's public product knowledge. When you open chat, it connects to our assistant hosted through ChatGPT Sites. Essential security cookies may be used by that hosting service. Chat does not depend on accepting optional website analytics.

When you send a question, its text and recent conversation context are sent to OpenAI to generate a response. Please do not enter personal, sensitive or confidential information into the AI conversation. To improve Joule, we save question-and-answer pairs, the knowledge version used, response outcomes, optional helpfulness ratings and feedback, timestamps and a pseudonymous session identifier. Access to the review dashboard is restricted to the authorised COP Angel administrators. Obvious email addresses, telephone numbers, web links and UK postcodes are removed before these review records are stored. Redaction is imperfect: names and other personal information may remain, so these records are treated as potentially personal information. Review records expire after 30 days and are deleted at the next daily cleanup or chat/review activity. The team may turn recurring questions into reviewed public FAQs, removing personal details before approval; approved knowledge improvements are retained until updated or removed. OpenAI processes API data under its own data and retention policies; disabling response storage does not remove all provider security or abuse-monitoring retention.

To control abuse and spending, the assistant stores request times, usage costs and pseudonymous session and network identifiers. These usage records are separate from the question-and-answer review records. Review records do not store IP addresses. Records older than 40 days are removed during subsequent chat activity. Hosting providers may also maintain security and operational logs.

If you choose Speak, your browser's speech service processes the microphone audio and may send it to its provider. You can check and edit the resulting text before sending it to the AI. Voice input is optional and availability depends on your browser.

The separate Contact the team form asks for your question, email address and telephone number. These fields are submitted through FormSubmit to info@copangel.com so we can respond; that form does not send them to the AI. FormSubmit's spam check and confirmation open separately. A chat answer is not confirmation that an enquiry has been delivered.

Optional website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 only after you accept analytics. It helps us understand visits, traffic sources, devices, page views, section engagement and enquiry activity. Analytics cookies may then be set on your device.

Our custom analytics events contain section names and enquiry categories. We do not send the names, email addresses or messages entered into our enquiry forms to Analytics. A form-submission attempt or a visit to the thank-you page does not prove that an email was delivered to our inbox.

Google processes analytics information to provide the service. See Google's privacy policy.

Your choice

You can accept or reject analytics. Your choice is remembered in this browser for up to 180 days using local storage. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the website or submitting enquiries. You can change your choice using the Cookie settings button at the bottom of each page.

Withdrawing consent stops further Analytics loading in this browser and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies. It does not automatically delete information already processed by Google.
